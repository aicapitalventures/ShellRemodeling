## 9. Approval, Adoption, Canon Lock

- **Founder Approved** means an authorized founder has affirmatively approved a record/concept.
- **Adopted** means it may govern downstream architecture within its scope.
- **Canon-Locked** is stronger: downstream work must preserve it unless an authorized superseding record explicitly unlocks/replaces it.
- Appearance in a draft is never adoption.

## 10. Revision and Supersession Law

Every controlling change must preserve version, prior status, supersession linkage, material change summary, authority, and effective date. Superseded records remain traceable.

## 11. Fact / Assumption / Decision Law

Where material, classify information as confirmed fact, founder-supplied fact, working assumption, recommendation, decision requiring adoption, externally verifiable fact, or unresolved matter.

Do not convert assumptions into facts, recommendations into decisions, intentions into actions, available credit into company capital, planned filings into a legal entity, or contract value/deposits into profit.
