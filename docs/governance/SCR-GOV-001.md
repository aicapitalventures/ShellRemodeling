## Proposed Major Decisions Requiring Explicit Approval

Final thresholds are unresolved, but the following should not be left to informal assumption:
- borrowing or guaranteeing debt
- issuing/transferring equity
- changing ownership percentages
- opening/closing bank or credit accounts
- purchasing/selling major assets
- changing legal entity form
- entering leases or long-term obligations
- related-party transactions
- litigation settlement above an adopted threshold
- adding a new business line with material regulatory/capital exposure
- dissolution, merger, sale, or substantially all asset transfer

## Capital Contributions

No founder contribution, available personal credit, or prior Carfixd commitment is automatically Shell & Co company capital. Contributions must be separately documented as equity, loan, reimbursable expense, or another adopted category.

## Owner Compensation and Distributions

Ownership percentage does not by itself determine payment for owner labor. The final system should separately govern:
- compensation for labor/services
- reimbursement of approved expenses
- tax/reserve allocations
- retained earnings
- distributable profit
- ownership distributions

No Build Room 00 record authorizes distributions.

## Equipment and Assets

Existing founder-owned tools remain founder property unless a written record transfers, leases, contributes, or otherwise authorizes company use. The company should maintain an equipment inventory and ownership/use status ledger.

## Digital Assets

Company-created domains, websites, business email, phone numbers, CRM, social profiles, ad accounts, brand source files, and customer databases should be controlled as company assets under later adopted governance. Personal credentials must not be stored publicly.

## Customer and Company Opportunity

Customer relationships generated under Shell & Co should be treated as company relationships under the final governance system. Any non-solicitation/company-opportunity rule must be implemented through valid legal documents rather than assumed from this architecture.
