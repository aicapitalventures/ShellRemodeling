## Genuine Operational Fundability

Shell & Co should improve fundability by becoming a better real business: accurate entity information, clean records, actual customers, real revenue, disciplined job economics, supplier relationships tied to business need, responsible payment history, cash reserves, and documented financial performance.

## Tradeline-Chasing Prohibition

The company must not treat artificial or unnecessary tradeline acquisition as a substitute for operations. Vendor accounts should exist because they serve a legitimate procurement/operational purpose and their terms are economically sensible.

## Available Credit Law

Personal or founder credit capacity is not company capital unless a documented legal transaction makes it so. No BFPS implementation step authorizes borrowing, personal guarantees, credit applications, or vendor-credit applications without a separate authorization gate.
