## Work Classification

Every estimate/scope should classify work into one or more of these buckets:

### A. Founder / Self-Performed Work
Work that founders are competent and legally authorized to perform under the applicable jurisdiction and project conditions.

### B. Employee-Performed Work
Reserved until employment, payroll, workers’ compensation, safety, supervision, and legal requirements are satisfied.

### C. Subcontracted Work
Work assigned to qualified independent businesses/trades under written scope, insurance/credential checks where appropriate, payment terms, quality expectations, and documentation.

### D. Regulated / Licensed-Trade Work
Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, structural, or other work that requires licensed/registered professionals or permits under the applicable jurisdiction must be routed accordingly. Build Room 00 does not assert which specific tasks require licensing in every jurisdiction.

### E. Permit-Controlled Work
No project may bypass a required permit merely because the customer requests speed or lower cost.

### F. Reserved / Not Yet Authorized
Any service not adequately understood, insured, permitted, staffed, or lawfully performable remains closed.

## Scope Discipline

Every project must define:
- included work
- excluded work
- owner/customer-supplied items
- allowances, if used
- demolition/disposal responsibility
- permit responsibility
- subcontracted/regulated work
- assumptions/unknown conditions
- change-order triggers
- payment triggers
- schedule dependencies

## Prohibited Shortcuts

- performing work known to require unavailable credentials
- accepting undocumented scope expansion
- hiding subcontracted work from project records
- promising timelines without dependency assumptions
- misrepresenting AI-generated imagery as project proof
- quoting regulated/compliance claims from memory as fact
