## Paid Lead Response Standard

Ads should run only during windows when Bernard or Elijah can monitor new leads.

Target the first human contact attempt within 15 minutes after a paid lead is received. If unanswered, call or text during the initial attempt, make a second attempt approximately two hours later, and make the final attempt on the next operating day.

## Qualification

Use the captured information and customer conversation to assess service-area fit, bathroom/remodel scope fit, property authority, budget context, timing, and ability to conduct a site review.

Qualified leads move to site-review handoff. Unqualified leads require a disposition reason and are closed.

## Site Review

Move site review through Recommended, Scheduled, then Completed or Declined.

## Estimate

After a site review, move the estimate to Preparing. When a written estimate is sent, mark it Sent and set the first follow-up for approximately 48 hours. If it remains open, target a second human follow-up approximately seven days after the estimate.

These are internal operating standards, not public customer promises.
