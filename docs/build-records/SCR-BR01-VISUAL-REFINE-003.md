## Founder Direction

Elijah L. Cooley requested that the live founder-review prototype retain the adopted gray / beige / white / charcoal identity while presenting it with substantially greater depth, contrast, definition, and visual impact. Specific requested refinements:

- make the approved horizontal header logo larger and allow the wording to command more of the upper-left header space;
- make the large approved stacked logo fill more of the right-side hero presentation box;
- deepen the gray, beige, white, and charcoal presentation so the site does not feel pastel, washed out, or visually weak;
- preserve the exact approved core S&C + diamond-blade artwork rather than redesigning the adopted logo.

Bernard Shell Jr. subsequently reviewed the live founder prototype, expressed a strong positive reaction, and agreed with Elijah L. Cooley on these visual changes. Accordingly, this visual direction is jointly founder-approved while the broader Build Room 01 lock remains pending completion of the Pre-Build Founder Intake.

## Applied Prototype Refinements

The founder-review stylesheet now:

- deepens the neutral palette while preserving the adopted color family;
- increases contrast and perceived definition across header, hero, buttons, studio, and cards;
- enlarges the approved horizontal header lockup on desktop/tablet/mobile within responsive constraints;
- enlarges the approved stacked hero logo and reduces unused presentation-box space;
- adds controlled contrast/saturation/brightness filters to web presentation of approved raster logo assets without modifying the authoritative source artwork;
- strengthens hero charcoal depth using layered gradients;
- strengthens the Remodel Studio visually as the signature customer-experience product;
- preserves keyboard focus, touch-target, mobile, and reduced-motion accessibility improvements.
