### 3. Estimate / Appointment Protection
**FOUNDER-DELEGATED — RESOLVED.** Shell & Co shall not impose unnecessary blanket estimate fees.

Adopted model:
- initial phone/photo project review: no charge;
- confirmed on-site assessment appointment: **$49 reservation fee**;
- reservation fee is credited in full toward a signed Shell & Co project if hired within 30 days;
- customer may cancel/reschedule with at least 24 hours notice for refund or one transfer;
- late cancellation/no-show: reservation fee is non-refundable;
- unusually intensive design, diagnostic or preconstruction work may carry a separately quoted fee disclosed before work begins.

Purpose: protect founder time and travel from ghost/no-show appointments while remaining fair to legitimate customers.

### 4. Customer Planning Budget Ranges

- $20,000–$35,000
- $35,000+
- Not sure yet

These bands reflect current national bathroom-remodel cost distributions while preserving Shell & Co’s individualized quoting model.

### 5. Project Deposit / Payment Schedule
**FOUNDER-DELEGATED — RESOLVED.** Adopt a fair anti-ghosting / anti-out-of-pocket structure:
- standard scheduling/mobilization deposit: **15% of signed contract price**;
- special-order/nonreturnable materials may require documented additional material funding or direct customer purchase when appropriate;
- progress payments are tied to written project milestones rather than arbitrary dates;
- final balance is tied to completion/punch-list closeout under the signed agreement;
- any jurisdiction-specific legal cap or stricter requirement overrides this default.

This project deposit is separate from the $49 on-site appointment reservation fee.

### 6. Accepted Payment Methods

- credit/debit card through the approved business processor;
- ACH/bank transfer through the approved business processor.

Do not accept personal or business checks. Electronic payments are not treated as cleared until the processor/bank confirms settlement. No payment method eliminates all fraud/chargeback risk; contracts, receipts, milestones and cleared-funds controls remain mandatory.

### 9. Travel Charge
**FOUNDER-DELEGATED — RESOLVED.** No travel surcharge through **35 one-way miles from Charlestown, Indiana**.

Beyond 35 miles: **$1.50 per additional one-way mile**. This approximates the round-trip vehicle-cost benchmark created by the July–December 2026 IRS business mileage rate of $0.76 per actual mile (2 × $0.76 = $1.52), rounded down for customer fairness.

Example: a site 50 miles away is 15 excess one-way miles → $22.50 travel surcharge.

Travel fee must be disclosed and accepted before an appointment is booked. The travel surcharge covers travel expense and is not automatically credited to the project; the separate $49 reservation fee is creditable as stated above.
