Customer Protection & Trust Architecture

Clear terms before money or promises move.

Appointments

Fair anti-no-show protection

Start with a free phone/photo review. A confirmed on-site assessment uses a $49 reservation fee, credited to a signed project within 30 days. Give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule; late cancellations/no-shows forfeit the fee.

Travel

35 miles included

No travel surcharge through 35 one-way miles from Charlestown, Indiana. Beyond that, the founder-selected policy is $1.50 per additional one-way mile, disclosed before the appointment is booked.

Payments

Cash, card or ACH

Checks are not accepted. Project contracts use a standard 15% scheduling/mobilization deposit, documented material needs and milestone-based progress payments, subject to stricter applicable legal requirements.

Financing

Acorn Finance selected

Acorn Finance is the founder-selected launch platform for third-party financing. Enrollment and the live customer financing link are still pending, so financing is not represented as currently active.

Warranty

Written terms, not vague promises

Shell & Co will not publish an unlimited satisfaction guarantee. Formal workmanship warranty language will activate only after its coverage, duration, exclusions and remedies are written and reviewed.

Privacy

Private customer data path

Production photo upload will explain how project images are used, where they are sent/stored, how long they are retained and how deletion requests work. Optional marketing consent will remain separate.
